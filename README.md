# poke-sparkle-pink

My romhack of Pokémon Red with new Pokémon, a female protagonist, balance changes, quality of life features, and much more!

Based on pokered-gbc.

## Handy files
- [**Pokédex**](POKEDEX.md)
- [**Type Chart**](TYPE_CHART.md)
- [**Move List**](MOVES.md)
- [**Trainer List**](TRAINERS.md)

## AI Disclosure

This romhack used Generative Large Language Models ("AI") to assist with writing code, as well as minor planning tasks. No AI was used to write dialogue text or draw graphics. The wiki files were generated with a script (tools/gen_wiki.py).

I made the game, AI just assisted me. But I understand if you would rather play a game where no AI was used at all.
